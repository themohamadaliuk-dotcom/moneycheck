Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

WorthChex is designed to provide free financial planning calculators and educational information. This privacy policy explains how information may be handled when you use worthchex.com.

1. Calculator information

The figures you enter into the WorthChex calculators are processed by the calculator in your browser to produce the requested result.

WorthChex does not require you to create an account or connect a bank account to use the calculators.

Calculator figures are not intentionally submitted to WorthChex as part of the calculator calculation itself.

2. Website usage and technical information

When you visit a website, technical information may be processed by hosting, security, advertising or other technology providers. This can include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, approximate location and information about how the website is used.

The exact information processed depends on the services active on the website and the choices you make through the site's consent controls.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

WorthChex uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertising where permitted by the applicable consent settings.

Google requires publishers using AdSense to provide users with privacy disclosures explaining relevant data collection, sharing and use associated with Google products and services, including advertising technologies such as cookies and identifiers.

More information about how Google uses data when you use partner websites can be found on Google's information page:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Consent and cookies

WorthChex uses Google's consent management technology to provide users with choices relating to advertising and certain cookies or similar technologies.

Depending on your location and the services being used, you may be asked to consent, decline consent or manage your advertising and privacy preferences.

Non-essential cookies or similar technologies may be subject to consent requirements. You can manage your preferences through the consent controls provided on the site.

5. Information we do not require

WorthChex does not require you to provide online banking credentials, bank account passwords or direct access to your bank account in order to use the calculators.

6. Third-party services

WorthChex may use third-party services to operate the website, deliver advertising, provide consent management, maintain security or understand website performance.

These providers may process technical or other information in accordance with their own privacy policies and the choices available through the site's consent system.

7. Your privacy choices

Where consent is required, WorthChex provides privacy choices through its consent management system. You can use those controls to review or change available advertising and consent preferences.

8. Data retention

WorthChex does not intentionally retain your calculator figures as part of the browser-based calculator calculation. Information processed by third-party services is subject to the relevant provider's policies and retention practices.

9. Children

WorthChex is not specifically directed at children and is intended as a general financial information and planning website.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

This policy may be updated when the website, advertising services, technologies or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was most recently updated.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions concerning WorthChex, please use the contact details provided by WorthChex on its official website.
