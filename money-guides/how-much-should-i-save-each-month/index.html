SAVINGS GUIDE

How much should I save each month?

There is no universal percentage or fixed amount. The best contribution is one that helps you move toward a goal without repeatedly damaging your monthly budget.

Start from your goal

Choose a target and deadline. If you have £2,000 and want £10,000 in two years, the basic gap is £8,000 over 24 months, or about £333.33 per month before interest.

Check affordability

Look at housing, bills, food, transport, debt and other regular costs first. Your savings contribution should leave enough room for ordinary spending and irregular costs.

Increase gradually

A practical approach is to start with a contribution you can maintain, then increase it after a pay rise or when a regular expense falls away. Consistency matters more than an impressive first month.
