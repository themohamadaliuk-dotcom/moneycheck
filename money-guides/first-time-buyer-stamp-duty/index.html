How does Stamp Duty work for first-time buyers?

First-time buyers in England and Northern Ireland may qualify for relief if they meet the conditions and the property price is within the relevant limit.

Current relief

For qualifying purchases up to £500,000, the current relief means no SDLT on the first £300,000 and 5% on the portion from £300,001 to £500,000. Purchases above £500,000 do not receive this first-time buyer relief under the current rules.

Check the details

Eligibility depends on whether all buyers are first-time buyers and whether the property and transaction meet the conditions. Additional-property and non-UK-resident rules can also affect the final liability.
