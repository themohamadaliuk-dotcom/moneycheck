What is disposable income?

Disposable income is the amount left after the regular expenses included in your calculation. It is useful because it shows your monthly breathing room before you make another financial commitment.

A simple formula

Take-home income minus the monthly costs you include equals disposable income. Depending on the purpose of the calculation, you might include housing, utilities, groceries, transport, subscriptions and debt payments.

Why the number matters

A positive result does not automatically mean a purchase is affordable. It may not include annual bills, irregular repairs, holidays or unexpected costs. A negative result is a clear warning that the expenses entered already exceed the income entered.

Use disposable income as one planning measure alongside savings, emergency funds and the total cost of a purchase or loan.
