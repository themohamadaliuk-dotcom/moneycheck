What is the take-home pay on £40,000?

Gross salary is not the same as the money you receive. Your net pay can change with tax region, National Insurance, pension contributions and student-loan deductions.

2026/27 tax basics

The standard Personal Allowance is £12,570. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland, the basic Income Tax rate is 20% on the first £37,700 of taxable income, with higher rates above that. Scotland has different income-tax bands. Employee National Insurance for most category A employees is 8% between the primary threshold and upper earnings limit, then 2% above it. citeturn816133search0turn816133search3

Why your payslip can differ

Pension contributions, salary sacrifice, student-loan repayments and your NI category can change the result. Treat a headline estimate as a planning figure rather than an exact payslip prediction.
