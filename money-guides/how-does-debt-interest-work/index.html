DEBT GUIDE

How does debt interest work?

Interest is one of the main reasons a debt balance can take longer to clear than the simple balance divided by the payment.

A simple monthly model

For an estimate, monthly interest can be represented as the remaining balance multiplied by the annual rate divided by 12. The payment then covers that interest first, with the rest reducing principal.

Why the early months matter

When the balance is large, the interest charge can be larger too. As the balance falls, the interest portion normally falls under a fixed-rate model.

That is why paying more earlier can have a stronger effect than waiting until the end of the agreement.
