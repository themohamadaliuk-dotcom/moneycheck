How to save £5,000 without making the plan unrealistic

A good savings plan starts with an amount you can repeat, not a number that looks impressive for one month.

Choose the deadline first

Divide £5,000 by the number of months you have. A 12-month target requires about £416.67 per month before interest or starting savings. A longer deadline reduces the monthly contribution.

Use what you already have

If you already have £1,000, the remaining goal is £4,000. The most useful plan is based on the gap, not the original target.

Make the contribution sustainable

Review fixed costs and recurring spending, but leave room for irregular expenses. If a contribution regularly forces you to dip back into savings, the target may be too aggressive.
