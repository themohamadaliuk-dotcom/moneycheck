How much Stamp Duty on a £300,000 house?

Stamp Duty Land Tax depends on the price and your buyer status. England and Northern Ireland use SDLT; Scotland and Wales have different property taxes.

Current standard bands

For a standard single residential property, current SDLT uses increasing portions of the price. The first £125,000 is charged at 0%, the next £125,000 at 2%, and the portion from £250,001 to £925,000 at 5%. GOV.UK also explains circumstances where a higher rate or relief applies.

Buyer status matters

First-time buyer relief can change the result, while additional-property and non-UK-resident surcharges can increase it.
