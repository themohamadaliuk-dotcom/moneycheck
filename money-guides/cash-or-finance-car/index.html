CAR FINANCE GUIDE

Should I pay cash or use finance for a car?

Neither option is automatically better. The right comparison is between the total cost, your available savings and the monthly breathing room you keep.

When cash can make sense

Paying cash avoids borrowing interest and keeps a finance payment off your monthly budget. But do not look only at the purchase price. Check how much savings remain afterwards and whether the purchase would leave you without a sensible emergency buffer.

When finance can make sense

Finance can preserve cash for emergencies and spread the cost. The trade-off is that APR, fees and the length of the agreement can make the total amount repaid substantially higher than the purchase price.

Compare four numbers

Look at the deposit, monthly payment, total amount repaid and total interest. Then compare the monthly payment with your disposable income rather than with your gross salary.
