MORTGAGE GUIDE

Is mortgage overpayment worth it?

For a repayment mortgage, an extra payment can reduce the balance sooner, which can reduce future interest and potentially shorten the term.

What an overpayment changes

Interest is calculated against the outstanding balance. Reducing that balance earlier can therefore reduce future interest under a fixed-rate model. The effect is generally stronger when the remaining balance and interest rate are higher.

Check your mortgage terms

Lenders can have annual overpayment limits, early repayment charges or other conditions. Check your mortgage documents before making a large extra payment.
