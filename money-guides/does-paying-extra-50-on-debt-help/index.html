DEBT GUIDE

Does paying £50 extra a month on debt help?

It can make a meaningful difference because the balance falls faster, which can reduce the amount of future interest charged.

Why a small increase matters

Interest is generally calculated from the remaining balance. Paying extra reduces that balance sooner, so future interest has a smaller amount to work from. The impact is larger when the APR is high or the remaining term is long.

Compare before and after

Use your current balance, APR and payment as the baseline. Then increase the monthly payment by £50 and compare the estimated months and total interest.

The useful question is not whether £50 sounds large. It is whether the extra payment is affordable and whether the interest saving is worth the impact on your wider budget.
