DEBT GUIDE

How long does it take to pay off £5,000 of debt?

The answer depends heavily on the interest rate and the payment you can sustain each month.

The repayment mechanics

Interest is added to the remaining balance, then your payment reduces the balance. At 0% interest, £5,000 paid at £200 a month takes 25 months. With interest, the timeline is longer and the total cost is higher.

Why APR matters

A higher APR means more of each early payment can go toward interest. If your payment is not greater than the interest being added, the balance will not fall under a simple amortisation model.

Use the actual APR and monthly payment from your account where possible, then compare what happens if you pay a little more.
