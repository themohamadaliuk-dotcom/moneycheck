MORTGAGE GUIDE

What is a 10% deposit on a £300,000 house?

A 10% deposit is £30,000. That leaves a £270,000 mortgage before taking account of other buying costs.

Why the percentage matters

A £30,000 deposit on a £300,000 property means an initial loan-to-value ratio of 90%. A larger deposit can reduce the mortgage amount and may give access to different lender pricing, although actual offers depend on the lender and applicant.

Do not budget only for the deposit

Buying a home can also involve tax, legal costs, surveys, moving costs, lender fees and insurance. Keep enough cash available for those costs instead of putting every pound into the deposit.
