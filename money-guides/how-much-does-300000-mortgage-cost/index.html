MORTGAGE GUIDE

How much does a £300,000 mortgage cost?

The monthly payment depends on the interest rate, mortgage type and term. The same borrowing amount can produce very different totals.

What changes the monthly payment?

Repayment mortgages include both interest and principal. A longer term usually lowers the monthly payment but can increase total interest. Interest-only borrowing can have a lower monthly interest payment but normally leaves the principal outstanding at the end of the term.

Example

At a 4.5% annual rate over 25 years, a £300,000 repayment mortgage has a significantly different monthly cost from the same balance over 35 years. The exact figures should be recalculated whenever the rate or term changes.
