AFFORDABILITY GUIDE

How much car can I afford?

The useful question is not simply whether a lender will approve the finance. It is whether the car fits your budget while leaving room for normal life and unexpected costs.

Start with your monthly budget

Begin with take-home income, then subtract the regular costs that actually apply to you: housing, utilities, food, transport, debt payments, subscriptions and other commitments. The money left is your disposable income for planning.

Then consider the full cost of the car. A finance payment is only one part of ownership; insurance, fuel, servicing, tyres, parking and repairs can all matter. A cheaper car with lower running costs can be easier to live with than a more expensive car that technically fits the finance payment.

Cash or finance?

For a cash purchase, ask how much of your savings would remain afterwards and whether you would still have an emergency buffer. For finance, look at the deposit, APR, term and monthly payment, then ask what percentage of your disposable income the payment consumes.
