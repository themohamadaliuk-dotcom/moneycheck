MORTGAGE GUIDE

How much mortgage can I afford?

A lender's maximum is not the same as a comfortable household budget. Start with what your income can support after the costs you already have.

Look at monthly affordability

Take-home income matters more to household budgeting than gross salary alone. Consider housing costs, bills, food, transport, debt and other commitments before adding a mortgage payment.

Deposit and rate matter

A larger deposit can reduce the amount borrowed and the loan-to-value ratio. A lower rate can reduce the monthly repayment, while a longer term can reduce the monthly payment but increase the total interest paid.

Use two checks

Use the Affordability Calculator to understand your monthly breathing room, then use the Mortgage Calculator to model the borrowing itself.
