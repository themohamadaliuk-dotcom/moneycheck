BUDGET GUIDE

What should I include in a monthly budget?

A useful budget includes the recurring costs that genuinely apply to you and makes room for less frequent expenses rather than pretending they do not exist.

Start with essentials

List housing, council tax where applicable, utilities, groceries, transport, insurance, childcare and minimum debt payments. Then add recurring discretionary costs such as subscriptions or memberships.

Do not forget irregular costs

Car servicing, annual insurance, school costs, gifts and repairs may not appear every month. A monthly budget can handle these by setting aside a regular amount for future bills.

Keep the budget useful

A budget that takes hours to maintain may not last. Focus on categories that change decisions. For affordability checks, the WorthChex calculator lets blank categories stay blank rather than silently assuming they are zero.
