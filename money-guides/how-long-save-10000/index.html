SAVINGS GUIDE

How long does it take to save £10,000?

The timeline depends mainly on what you already have and what you can save consistently each month.

The simple calculation

Subtract your current savings from £10,000. Then divide the remaining amount by your monthly contribution and round up to the next whole month. For example, if you have £2,000 and save £250 each month, £8,000 remains and the simple timeline is 32 months.

What can change the answer?

Interest can shorten the timeline if the rate is positive and maintained. One-off deposits can also help. On the other hand, missed contributions, withdrawals or changes in income can make the actual date later.

Use 0% interest when you want a pure savings-contribution timeline. Use an interest assumption only when it reflects a realistic rate for the account or scenario you are modelling.
