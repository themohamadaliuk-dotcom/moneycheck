SAVINGS GUIDE

How long does it take to save £500 a month?

At £500 per month, your savings grow by £6,000 per year before interest. The exact time to a target depends on how much you already have.

Simple examples

With no starting savings and no interest, £5,000 takes 10 months, £10,000 takes 20 months and £20,000 takes 40 months. If you already have £2,000, subtract that from the target before dividing by £500.

Interest and consistency

Interest can reduce the time slightly when the rate is positive and contributions continue. Real accounts can have changing rates, so treat an interest-based projection as an illustration.

The most important variable is whether £500 remains affordable every month. If a contribution is too high to maintain, the theoretical deadline is not very useful.
