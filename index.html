ABOUT WORTHCHEX

Useful money tools without the spreadsheet.

WorthChex was created to make everyday financial decisions easier to understand.

Whether you're considering a purchase, building savings or paying down debt, the aim is simple: give you a quick way to explore the numbers before making a decision.

You don't need an account, you don't need to connect your bank and you don't need to understand complicated financial formulas.

IMPORTANT

A calculator is a starting point, not a verdict.

WorthChex calculators provide estimates for educational and informational purposes only.

Results depend on the information you enter and may not reflect your complete financial situation. Irregular expenses, future commitments, fees, taxes, interest changes and other circumstances can affect real-world affordability.

WorthChex does not provide personalised financial, investment, mortgage, tax or debt advice. Consider your own circumstances and seek qualified professional advice where appropriate.
