FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I leave an affordability expense blank?

Yes. Leave it blank if it genuinely does not apply to you or you don't want to include it yet. WorthChex excludes blank categories instead of treating them as confirmed £0 spending.

Is the affordability score financial advice?

No. It is a simplified planning indicator based on your entered figures and cannot know every part of your financial circumstances.

How accurate is the debt payoff calculator?

It is an estimate based on your balance, APR and monthly payment. Actual lender calculations can differ because of fees, payment timing, variable rates and account rules.
