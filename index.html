IMPORTANT

A calculator is a starting point, not a guarantee.

WorthChex provides estimates for educational and informational purposes. Results depend on the information you enter and may not reflect every expense, commitment, fee, tax, interest charge or change in your circumstances.

Affordability results should not be treated as a promise that a purchase, loan or finance agreement is suitable for you. For debt calculations, actual lender results can differ because of payment dates, interest calculations, fees and other terms.

If you're struggling to keep up with essential payments or debt, consider getting free, confidential debt guidance rather than relying solely on a calculator.
