HOW WE CALCULATE WorthChex methodology We aim to make each calculator understandable enough that you can see what the result means and what assumptions sit behind it. Affordability Disposable income = income − entered monthly expenses Blank expense categories are excluded. Cash purchases are compared with disposable monthly income; financed purchases are assessed using their monthly payment. Savings Months = remaining goal ÷ monthly contribution The number of months is rounded up to the next whole month. The estimate does not assume savings interest or investment returns. Debt Monthly interest = balance × APR ÷ 12 Each month, estimated interest is added and the payment reduces the remaining balance. The calculation stops when the balance reaches zero.
