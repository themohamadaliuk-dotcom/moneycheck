CURRENT 2026/27 RULES

Built around the published residential bands.

Standard residential SDLT applies in increasing portions of the property price. Qualifying first-time buyers receive relief up to £500,000, while higher rates can apply to additional properties. A non-UK-resident surcharge may also apply.

Special transactions such as companies, trusts, linked purchases, multiple dwellings, shared ownership and certain leasehold transactions can follow different rules.
