CURRENT RULES

What this estimate covers

For a standard residential purchase, the current bands used here are 0% up to £125,000, 2% on the next £125,000 and 5% on the next £675,000, with higher bands above that. Qualifying first-time buyers pay 0% up to £300,000 and 5% on the portion from £300,001 to £500,000.

Additional-property purchases can attract a surcharge. Complex transactions, linked purchases, companies and some special cases can follow different rules.
