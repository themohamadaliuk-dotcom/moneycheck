HOW IT WORKS

What does affordability actually mean?

Affordability is not simply whether you technically have enough money for a purchase. It is about how the purchase affects the money remaining after the costs you have entered.

WorthChex only counts expense categories you enter. If you don't pay rent, leave rent blank. If you don't cover a particular category, leave it blank. Blank categories are not silently treated as £0.

For a cash purchase, the purchase price affects the current month's budget. For finance, the monthly finance payment is considered as the ongoing commitment.
