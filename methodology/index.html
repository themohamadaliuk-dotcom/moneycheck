METHODOLOGY

How WorthChex calculates its results.

WorthChex uses transparent formulas and clearly stated assumptions. Results are estimates based on the information entered and are not personalised financial advice.

AFFORDABILITY

Cash and finance are separate scenarios.

Monthly breathing room

Disposable income = take-home income − entered recurring expenses

Only entered expense categories are included. Blank categories are not treated as confirmed £0 spending.

Cash purchase

Savings after purchase = current savings − purchase price

The cash purchase is checked against the savings buffer separately from monthly disposable income.

Finance purchase

Monthly breathing room after finance = disposable income − modelled monthly payment

The finance payment is calculated from the amount financed, APR, term and optional extra payment.

SAVINGS

Goal planning and growth.

Monthly mode

The calculator advances month by month using the selected interest assumption until the target is reached or the planning horizon is exhausted.

Target-date mode

The required monthly contribution is solved from the target, current savings, time remaining and selected growth assumption.

0% safety case

Future value = current savings + contribution × number of contribution periods
DEBT & BORROWING

Amortisation models.

Debt payoff

Monthly interest = balance × APR ÷ 12

The payment is applied after monthly interest. If the payment does not exceed the interest, the model correctly reports that the balance will not fall.

Mortgage & personal loan

Payment = principal × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)^−n)

Repayment borrowing uses standard amortisation. Optional overpayments are modelled as additional monthly principal reduction.

Interest-only mortgage

The estimate uses monthly interest payments while keeping the principal outstanding at the end of the term.

TAX & PROPERTY

Published UK rules are used for the current year.

Take-home pay

The 2026/27 calculator uses published Income Tax, employee National Insurance and student-loan thresholds. Scotland uses its own published Income Tax bands.

Stamp Duty

The residential SDLT calculator is scoped to England and Northern Ireland and includes first-time buyer, additional-property and non-UK-resident options in the standard model.

GOV.UK residential SDLT rates

LIMITATIONS

What the calculators cannot know.

WorthChex cannot automatically know irregular spending, future rate changes, fees, lender rules, tax-code changes, benefits, investment performance or personal priorities.

Use the calculators as planning and comparison tools. Important decisions should be checked against official guidance, the actual lender/property documentation or a qualified professional.
