DEBT EXPLAINED

How does debt interest affect repayment?

Interest is added to the balance according to the simplified monthly calculation used by the calculator. Your payment then reduces the remaining balance.

If the monthly payment is not greater than the interest being added, the balance will not fall under this model.

The result is an estimate. Actual lender calculations may differ because of fees, payment timing, variable rates and account-specific rules.
