How the estimate is calculated

WorthChex uses the standard repayment-mortgage amortisation formula. The estimate assumes a constant interest rate and regular monthly payments throughout the term.

Actual mortgages can differ because of fees, fixed-rate periods, overpayments, product changes and lender-specific terms.
