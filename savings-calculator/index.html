SAVINGS EXPLAINED

How much should I save each month?

There isn't one monthly savings amount that works for everyone. A useful target is one that you can repeat consistently without putting essential costs or your wider budget under unnecessary pressure.

WorthChex calculates the amount still required and divides it by your monthly contribution. The answer is rounded up to a whole month.

This basic estimate doesn't assume investment returns, savings interest, missed payments or changes to your monthly saving amount.
